# Role card art

`role-emblems.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. The atlas contains four equally sized quadrants, sampled by UI UVs: top-left Mafia, top-right Doctor, bottom-left Detective, bottom-right Villager.

Prompt: A production square 2x2 sprite atlas, no gaps or text; gilded engraved tarot emblems on midnight navy textured leather, antique brass double borders. Mafia: ivory half mask, crossed daggers, crimson silk. Doctor: healing vial and botanical sprig, emerald. Detective: magnifying glass, eye and secret letter, blue. Villager: wheat sheaf and lantern, amber. Consistent scale, front-on board-game illustration, clear silhouettes, no watermark or mockup scene.

The images are not secret. Only the assignment of a role to a living player is private.
